SMS Expense Tracker

An Android app that reads your bank and UPI SMS on your phone, sorts them into categories and shows what you spent. It has no internet permission: your messages and money never leave the phone.

Version 1.5

21 November 2026 · The largest update so far, and the first version signed for release.

Coming from 1.4 or earlier? Those were test builds, so 1.5 installs as a new app. Before you uninstall the old one, use Settings › Your data › Back up all data (available in the latest test build), then restore the file in 1.5. Nothing is lost.

New

  • Money habits. Patterns in your own spending: weekends against weekdays, small spends, category changes and cash, with simple charts and a note on how complete your data is. Worked out on your phone; it describes, it never advises.
  • Card bills. Statements and due dates from your bank's SMS, and whether each bill looks paid.
  • Meal and prepaid cards. Loads count as income once the card is saved; spends show as paid by prepaid card.
  • Notifications for new transactions, with Confirm and Exclude right there, and an optional evening summary.
  • Skipped messages (off by default): check the bank SMS the app left out, ten at a time.
  • Why it's not a transaction. Reasons in plain groups (Not a payment, Already counted, Not my spending). Optional, and the right ones teach the app to skip similar messages.
  • Share the app from Settings › About.
  • Back up all data to one file, and restore it on a new phone or version.

Better

  • Clearer merchants. One name per well-known shop (Zepto, Swiggy, Amazon…), with sub-brands such as Swiggy Instamart kept apart, and a bank's "To Block, call…" line is never mistaken for a merchant.
  • More messages understood: brokerage and SIP money, UPI addresses as payees, card slips with a city.
  • Scan a whole year or your whole inbox. A transaction whose SMS you deleted is kept, and says so.
  • Hold a transaction to read its SMS. Excluded transactions can have a category too.
  • Overview totals and categories open exactly the transactions they count.
  • Reviewing: shorter cards, duplicates linked to their original, your own subcategories, names for UPI addresses, notes and a cleaner editor.
  • Haptics at the moments that matter, following your phone's touch feedback setting.
  • Small screens: rows, tags and buttons read in full on compact phones.

Fixed

  • SMS showed "Not connected" after an update.
  • A category changed from a notification could be lost.
  • A meal card load appeared twice in Review.
  • Skipped messages got stuck after a swipe.
  • A card spend showed the merchant "Block".
  • The "no category" list included excluded transactions.
  • Text was cut on narrow phones.

Version 1.0

The first version

How to update

  • Install the new version over the one you have. Don't uninstall first: your transactions live only on your phone, and uninstalling deletes them.
  • On first launch the app brings your saved transactions up to date with the new rules; anything you edited yourself is kept as you left it.
  • Settings › About shows the version you have, and "What's new" appears once after each update.

Privacy

  • The app has no internet permission. Reading, sorting and Money habits all happen on your phone.
  • Account and card numbers are masked before anything is stored.
  • "Remember my reviews" keeps only fingerprints of messages and your decisions, never the text of an SMS.
